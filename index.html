StarTab Privacy Policy

Last updated: May 2026

Data Collection

StarTab does not collect, transmit, or share any personal data. All user data (quick link settings and to-do notes) is stored locally in your browser using chrome.storage.local and never leaves your device.

Permissions

tabs: Used only to display your currently open tabs on the dashboard. Tab data is not stored or transmitted.

storage: Used to save your quick link preferences and to-do notes locally on your device.
